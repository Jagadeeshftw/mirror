Mirror 1.0.2, beta, for **Monad testnet** (chain 10143). Test funds only: nothing in this build touches mainnet or real money.

Mirror copies a Perpl trader for you through your own contract account, which checks your limits on every order and can never send funds anywhere but back to you.

## Changes since 1.0.1

- Mirror's hosted testnet service is live: the copy engine and relayer run on Railway, so the demo trades, creating an onchain account, deposits and following work.
- If the service is ever unreachable, the app says so plainly ("isn't reachable right now"), and watch mode shows the demo account's history from Mirror's indexer topped up with the newest blocks read from Monad.
- Copies whose timing is unknown no longer show "0.00 s".
- **Max** (Send, Follow) and **Cap room** (Funds) fill in the exact amount. They used to round to two decimals, so a wallet holding 99.996 test AUSD got 100.00, more than it held, and Send stayed disabled.

## Changes in 1.0.1

- With Mirror's service down, the Demo screen ("Watch real copies") reads the demo account's copies straight from Monad instead of showing an error.
- Account data on screen refreshes reliably on a slow network.

## What's in it

- **One passkey, no seed phrase.** Create your account with one fingerprint prompt (Mera passkeys through Google Password Manager); restore it on another phone signed in to the same Google account.
- **Watch before you deposit.** Copies landing on the team-run demo account, with the leader's fill, your fill, the difference in basis points and both transactions. When Mirror's service is not reachable, the copies are read straight from Monad and the Run buttons say why they are off.
- **Follow with your own limits.** Leverage cap, size ratio, per-market caps, entry filter against the leader's onchain entry, daily loss and drawdown stops, per-leader budgets and loss stops. Every rule lives in your contract; a copy that breaks one is recorded onchain as Blocked, with the numbers.
- **Stops anyone can execute.** Stop-loss and take-profit levels that any wallet can trigger once they are true onchain, so they work even if Mirror is down; the caller is paid nothing.
- **Stop following, keep my positions.** Enforced by your contract: it refuses every further copy from that leader, opens and closes, while your own stops and closes keep working.
- **Several leaders, one deposit,** each with its own budget and loss stop.
- **What it costs, before you sign.** Mirror is Perpl builder 26: 0.02% of the size a copy opens, nothing on closes, never above the maximum you sign. Shown before the passkey prompt and on every copy.
- **Alerts only you can read.** Sealed to a second key from your passkey; the push service only sees "New activity".
- **Share cards and friend suggestions** for a take-profit or stop-loss, which you accept as an onchain level.
