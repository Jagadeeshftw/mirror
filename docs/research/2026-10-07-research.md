## 2. Best in class outside the hackathon

Sources are in the research agent's notes, linked below. Mobile-app details for fomo are secondary reporting.

**What their users rely on that Mirror lacks:**
1. **A choice for existing positions.** Binance offers copy all, "copy better entries only", or skip ([Binance FAQ](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/frequently-asked-questions-on-binance-futures-copy-trading-6ed0995daf0b42d5816beaf1e31ca09d)). eToro has a "Copy Open Trades" checkbox that opens at today's price ([eToro](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/how-it-works/)). Mirror's match-now opens at the current price with a slippage bound, but there's no "only if within X% of the leader's entry".
2. **A stop on the whole copy per leader.** eToro's copy stop-loss defaults to 40%. Mirror has this per account; with one account per leader it is effectively per leader.
3. **Pause vs stop.** Pause keeps positions synced; stop asks whether to close or keep. Mirror has pause and close-all, but no "stop and keep".
4. **Leader stats depth and a risk score that can't be gamed.** eToro's 7-day-averaged score ([eToro](https://etoro.com/en-us/news-and-analysis/etoro-updates/risk-score-what-it-is-and-how-its-calculated)). Copier PnL and AUM shown on profiles.
5. **Several leaders with separate budgets.** Binance allows 20, eToro 100.
6. **A social loop and notifications.** fomo is "follow → alert → mirror manually", with a verified-PnL feed and sharing ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/405563/crypto-trading-app-fomo-raises-75-million-at-550-million-valuation-in-index-ventures-led-series-b-report)). Notably, fomo **does not auto-copy**.
7. **Leader payouts.** eToro pays leaders 1.5% a year on AUM, only while their risk score stays under 8. OKX uses a true high-water mark.

**What they do badly that we can do visibly better:**
1. **Custody.**
   - CEXs hold the funds.
   - API-key copiers leak keys: 3Commas, $20M+ ([BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/crypto-platform-3commas-admits-hackers-stole-api-keys)).
   - Telegram bots hold keys: Maestro, Unibot, Banana Gun exploits.
   - Hyperliquid agent keys can't withdraw but can trade the whole account with no limits ([HL docs](https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/for-developers/api/nonces-and-api-wallets)).
   - Mirror's keeper can't withdraw **and** is bounded by an onchain policy.
2. **Risk settings that only live in the copier's server.** Every incumbent enforces them off-chain, where they can drift. Mirror enforces them on every order and logs every block.
3. **Opaque leaderboards and survivorship bias.** Bitget's "Elite" badge needs only 40 USDT profit in 7 days. Mirror's leader data is all onchain, including dead accounts, and can show **copier-realized** PnL.
4. **Copy lag and different fills.** One cited study: 97% of leaders profitable but only about 43% of followers (Arkham; unverified). Mirror can publish the measured fill gap and latency of every copy.
5. **Pooled vault losses.** HLP's JELLY and POPCAT losses ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/large-popcat-trades-result-in-usd5-million-loss-for-hyperliquid-vault)). Mirror's accounts are segregated.
6. **Silent desync in off-chain copiers.** Mirror drives each follower toward a target state checked against onchain positions.

## C. What I recommend not adding, and why

- **Leader fee share via builder codes:** a contract change plus Perpl approval plus payout logic, all before a freeze in a few days. The story ("leaders get paid") can be told as the path to a business without shipping it.
- **The full social layer (friends, chat, TP/SL suggestions):** Cult already owns social. Done half-way it would look worse than not having it. Keep share cards as the social surface.
- **MetaMask plugin and Aurora deposits:** each adds a bounty entry but splits a solo week. Aurora has no AUSD route and needs a swap.
- **More contract features** (fixed sizing, per-leader stops in a pooled account, onchain high-water mark): the contracts are the best-tested part of Mirror. Changing them now puts that at risk for marginal gains.
- **AI agents as a feature:** keep it as a line in the pitch (a leader can be an agent; the limits are the trust layer), not a build.

## D. Proposed final feature set

**Contract changes:** none. The contracts as they stand (policy, match-now, blocked events, signed actions, gasless deposits) are the frozen core. Optional and only if Perpl grants a builder code before the freeze: builder attribution via `execOrdersV2`.

**Everything else:**
1. Testnet deployment, hosted engine and indexer, public APK, watch mode (A1)
2. Copy-quality proof on the stats page and in the feed (A2)
3. "What if I had followed" backtest with the follower's limits (A3)
4. Shareable proof cards (A4)
5. Encrypted alerts outside the app (A5, needs Firebase)
6. Adversarial guards and the adversarial walk-through doc (A6)
7. Part 5 fixes B1–B6, then the videos (B7)
8. If room: multi-leader budget split (A7), match-now entry filter and "stop and keep" (A8), Perpl analytics view (A9)
