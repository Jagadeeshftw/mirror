Design proposal · Android · v1

Mirror app screens

Copy the best traders on Perpl from an Android phone, with limits a smart contract enforces on every copied order. Below: 12 screens plus 3 empty and error states, 27 states in all, each drawn in light and dark at 390 px.

Principles

  • Numbers lead. Mono, tabular figures for every amount, address and hash.
  • Green and red mean PnL, side or state. Never decoration.
  • AUSD balance is visible top right on every main screen.
  • No motion anywhere in the app. State changes are drawn, not animated.
  • Android patterns: bottom navigation, bottom sheets, system back, system passkey sheets.

01

Welcome and create account

One screen, one decision. The example copy card shows the product before any explanation does.

  1. Welcome
  2. Android passkey sheet
01a

Welcome

Purpose
First launch. Says what the app does and the custody promise in two lines.
Interactions
Create account starts passkey creation. I already have an account opens restore. No email, seed phrase or wallet extension.
01b

Android passkey sheet

Purpose
The system Credential Manager sheet, styled as Android draws it, not in our colours, so it reads as the OS.
Interactions
Create saves the passkey and asks for a fingerprint. Cancel returns to Welcome with nothing created. The account is ready when the sheet closes.
02

Restore on a new phone

The account lives on Monad. The passkey syncs through Google Password Manager. Nothing to import.

  1. Passkey picker
  2. Account found
  3. Balances restored
02a

Passkey picker

Purpose
The system lists the synced passkey for this app.
Interactions
Continue triggers the fingerprint prompt. Sign in another way shows a QR code for a passkey kept on another device.
02b

Account found

Purpose
Shows each restore step and its result, so the wait is explained.
Interactions
Read-only progress. Back cancels. Steps cover passkey, smart account, follow rules, then balances.
02c

Balances restored

Purpose
Confirms equity, follows and positions, and that copying never stopped.
Interactions
Go to Home. The old-device note links to the device list in Settings.
03

Home

Equity is the hero number. The AUSD balance chip sits top right on every main screen.

03

Home

Full-length capture
Purpose
Answers how am I doing and is it working: equity, today's PnL, active follows, latest copies.
Interactions
Balance chip opens the account breakdown. Add funds and Withdraw open their flows. Rows open the leader or the feed item. Avatar opens Account controls.
04

Add funds

Two steps: receive AUSD on Monad, then deposit into the account with one signed permit.

  1. Receive AUSD
  2. Deposit with permit
04a

Receive AUSD

Purpose
Address and QR for receiving AUSD from an exchange or another wallet.
Interactions
Copy and Share the address. The address is grouped in fours so it can be checked by eye. Deposit appears once AUSD arrives.
04b

Deposit with permit

Purpose
Moves AUSD from the wallet into the trading account, inside the beta cap.
Interactions
Max fills the lesser of wallet balance and cap room. The cap meter shows what this deposit uses. One passkey signature, no approval transaction, no gas.
05

Leaderboard

Ranked by a risk-adjusted score, not raw PnL, so the top of the list is the safest good trader.

05

Leaderboard

Full-length capture
Purpose
Find traders worth following. Each row shows return, drawdown, win rate, leverage, score, markets and Nansen labels.
Interactions
Period toggle 7D/30D/90D. Filter chips for max drawdown, markets and labeled wallets. Sort menu. Tapping a row opens the profile.
06

Leader profile

Full-length capture. A due-diligence page that states the risks as plainly as the returns.

06

Profile and due diligence

Full-length capture
Purpose
Decide whether to follow. Shows the equity curve with the drawdown marked, key stats, markets, trade frequency, Nansen labels, cross-venue history and risk flags.
Interactions
Period toggle. Copy address. Bell sets alerts without following. Follow opens the follow sheet with limits pre-set from this leader's risk.
07

Follow sheet

Every policy control in one sheet, each with plain consequences shown next to the number.

  1. Set limits (full length)
  2. Review and approve
07a

Set limits (full length)

Full-length capture
Purpose
All eight controls: allocation, sizing, max leverage, max notional per market, allowed markets, daily loss stop, high-water-mark stop, expiry.
Interactions
Steppers, sliders and chips. Each control previews its effect in AUSD. A warning appears when the leader regularly exceeds a limit. Review follow goes to step 2.
07b

Review and approve

Purpose
A last read of the exact rules the contract will enforce.
Interactions
Back edits. Approve with passkey signs once. Gas is sponsored. The follow is live on the next leader trade.
08

Live copy feed

Proof that it works, one order at a time: latency, commit state and a transaction link on every copy.

08

Live feed

Full-length capture
Purpose
Every leader trade and what happened to your copy.
Interactions
Filter by copied, blocked or closes. The three-dot track shows Proposed, Voted, Finalized. The hash opens monadvision.com/tx. A blocked item opens its detail sheet.
09

Blocked by your rule

A blocked copy is the product working as intended. It gets a clear explanation, not an error.

  1. Blocked items in the feed
  2. Detail sheet
09a

Blocked items in the feed

Purpose
The Blocked filter of the feed. Each item names the rule in one line: leverage, market, notional.
Interactions
Tap Details to open the explanation sheet. The transaction link shows the onchain rejection.
09b

Detail sheet

Purpose
Explains which rule blocked the copy, with both numbers, every rule that was checked, and the onchain rejection.
Interactions
Edit rule opens the follow sheet at Max leverage. Done closes. Your funds were not touched.
10

Positions and PnL

Every position traces back to the leader who opened it.

10

Positions

Full-length capture
Purpose
Total, unrealised and realised PnL, attribution per leader, open positions with entry, mark and liquidation price.
Interactions
Group by market or leader. Tap a position for its fills and the leader trade it copied. Close-all lives in Account controls.
11

Account controls

The user is always in charge: pause, close everything, withdraw. Withdrawals are gasless.

  1. Controls
  2. Close all confirmation
  3. Withdraw amount
  4. Gasless confirmation
  5. Withdrawal sent
11a

Controls

Purpose
One place for the emergency levers and per-follow pauses.
Interactions
Pause all, or pause one follow. Close all opens a confirmation. Withdraw opens the amount screen.
11b

Close all confirmation

Purpose
Following is already paused. The dialog lists what will close and the estimated result.
Interactions
Close all asks for the passkey and closes at market. Cancel leaves everything as is.
11c

Withdraw amount

Purpose
Withdraws to the user's own wallet, the only destination allowed.
Interactions
Keypad entry, 25%, 50% and Max chips. Withdrawable excludes margin held by open positions.
11d

Gasless confirmation

Purpose
Shows the fee as 0.00 MON and why.
Interactions
Confirm with passkey signs. The app sponsors gas.
11e

Withdrawal sent

Purpose
Confirms the amount, finality and the new balance.
Interactions
Transaction link. Done returns to Account.
12

Notifications and Settings

Push carries the same facts as the feed: what was copied, what was blocked, what stopped.

  1. Android push
  2. In-app notifications
  3. Settings
12a

Android push

Purpose
Lock-screen notifications for copies, blocks, stops and withdrawals.
Interactions
Tap opens the matching feed item or sheet. Notifications are grouped under the app.
12b

In-app notifications

Purpose
History of everything the app told you, by day.
Interactions
Filter by type. Unread items are marked with a dot. Tap to open.
12c

Settings

Full-length capture
Purpose
Passkeys, the linked trading key, theme, network and the contracts behind the app.
Interactions
Sign out an old phone. Revoke the trading key, which stops all copying. Theme: System, Light or Dark. Contract links open MonadVision.
13

Empty and error states

Each one says what is happening and what to do next.

  1. No follows yet
  2. Network error
  3. Withdrawal pending
13a

No follows yet

Purpose
Funded but idle. Points to the next step.
Interactions
Browse leaders opens the leaderboard. Two low-drawdown suggestions open profiles.
13b

Network error

Purpose
The app can't reach Monad. The banner says that copying continues onchain.
Interactions
Retry. The cached feed is dimmed and timestamped.
13c

Withdrawal pending

Purpose
Finality is slower than usual. Shows how far the transaction got.
Interactions
Leave safely, a push follows. View on MonadVision.
